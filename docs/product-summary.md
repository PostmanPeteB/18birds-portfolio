# Product Summary

18Birds is a multi-tenant golf scoring and administration platform built to support clubs, competitions, operational workflows, and scalable SaaS delivery.

## Problem space
Many clubs and operators rely on fragmented workflows, manual administration, and dated tooling. 18Birds is intended to provide a cleaner, more scalable operational platform.

## Product direction
The product is being developed as a structured multi-tenant SaaS platform with emphasis on:
- usability
- maintainability
- tenant-aware data separation
- modern deployment and delivery practices
